## 1. Legal & Regulatory Authorization
- [ ] Source operates within public domain, official publication, or open data mandate (e.g. India OGD, gazette).
- [ ] No scraping prohibitions, authentication bypass, or Terms of Service violations involved.
- [ ] Intellectual property and attribution constraints documented and accepted.
- [ ] Retaining raw artifacts in storage complies with applicable legal and statutory provisions.

## 2. Personal Data & Privacy Risks
- [ ] Source does not publish names of sexual assault victims or minors (compliance with Section 228A IPC / Section 73 BNS and POCSO Act).
- [ ] Personal telephone numbers, email addresses, and residential door numbers are absent or reliably strippable.
- [ ] Source narrative excerpts are verified free of identifiable domestic or medical disclosures.
- [ ] High-risk records can be systematically mapped to generalized administrative centroids.

## 3. Provenance & Technical Quality
- [ ] Unique, durable source identifiers exist for each record (e.g. petition/case number, gazette ID).
- [ ] Publication timestamps are explicitly differentiated from event occurrence timestamps.
- [ ] Machine-readable schema format (JSON, CSV, XML) or validated parser available.
- [ ] Refresh frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and expected volume documented.
- [ ] Error rate and schema drift monitoring strategy defined.
